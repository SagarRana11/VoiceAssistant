# NSTEMI

## Full Form

Non-ST Elevation Myocardial Infarction

## Clinical Meaning

NSTEMI is a type of heart attack caused by reduced or blocked blood flow to the heart muscle, resulting in damage to heart tissue.

## Patient Explanation

You had a mild to moderate heart attack where one of the heart arteries became partially blocked.

## Common Symptoms

- Chest pain or pressure
- Shortness of breath
- Sweating
- Nausea
- Fatigue
- Pain in jaw, arm, back

## Causes

- Coronary artery blockage
- Blood clot
- High cholesterol
- Smoking
- Diabetes
- High blood pressure

## Diagnosis

- ECG
- Troponin blood test
- Echocardiography
- Angiography

## Treatment

- Blood thinners
- Anti-clot medicines
- Statins
- Angiography
- Angioplasty / Stent if needed

## Risks if Untreated

- Larger heart attack
- Heart failure
- Irregular heartbeat
- Sudden cardiac arrest

## FAQ

Q: Is NSTEMI serious?  
A: Yes. It is a heart attack and needs urgent treatment.

Q: Can I recover?  
A: Many patients recover well with timely treatment and lifestyle changes.

## Keywords
