# Triple Vessel Disease

## Full Form

Triple Vessel Coronary Artery Disease

## Clinical Meaning

Triple vessel disease means all three major heart arteries have significant blockages.

## Patient Explanation

Three important blood vessels supplying your heart have narrowed or blocked areas. This reduces blood flow to multiple parts of the heart.

## Why It Is Serious

- Large area of heart at risk
- Higher heart attack risk
- Can reduce heart pumping
- Often needs advanced treatment

## Common Symptoms

- Chest pain
- Breathlessness
- Tiredness
- Reduced exercise tolerance
- Heart attack symptoms in some patients

## Risk Factors

- Diabetes
- Smoking
- High cholesterol
- High blood pressure
- Family history
- Long-standing CAD

## Diagnosis

- Coronary angiography
- Stress testing
- ECG
- Echocardiography

## Treatment

- Intensive medicines
- Bypass surgery often recommended
- Angioplasty in selected cases
- Risk factor control
- Rehabilitation

## Risks If Delayed

- Heart attack
- Heart failure
- Ongoing angina
- Sudden cardiac death

## Recovery Outlook

Many patients do well after timely revascularization and long-term care.

## FAQ

Q: Does everyone need bypass surgery?  
A: Not always, but many patients benefit from it.

Q: Is this severe disease?  
A: Yes, it usually requires specialist treatment.

Q: Can I recover?  
A: Yes, many patients recover with proper treatment.

## Voice Short Response

Three major heart arteries are significantly blocked. Timely treatment can greatly help.
