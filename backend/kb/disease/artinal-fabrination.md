# Atrial Fibrillation (AF)

## Full Form
Atrial Fibrillation

## Clinical Meaning
AF is an irregular and often fast heart rhythm caused by abnormal electrical activity in the upper chambers of the heart.

## Patient Explanation
Your heart rhythm has become uneven. The heart may beat too fast or irregularly, causing palpitations or tiredness.

## Why It Is Serious
- Increases stroke risk
- Can weaken heart over time
- Can cause dizziness
- Can worsen heart failure

## Common Symptoms
- Palpitations
- Irregular pulse
- Tiredness
- Breathlessness
- Dizziness
- Chest discomfort

## Risk Factors
- Age
- High blood pressure
- Heart disease
- Diabetes
- Thyroid problems
- Alcohol excess
- Sleep apnea

## Diagnosis
- ECG
- Holter monitor
- Echocardiography
- Blood tests

## Treatment
- Blood thinners
- Rate control medicines
- Rhythm medicines
- Cardioversion
- Ablation in selected patients

## Risks If Delayed
- Stroke
- Persistent rapid heart rate
- Heart weakness
- Recurrent symptoms

## Recovery Outlook
Many people live normal lives with proper treatment and monitoring.

## FAQ

Q: Why blood thinner needed?  
A: AF can form clots that may cause stroke.

Q: Can rhythm become normal again?  
A: Yes, in many patients.

Q: Is it life threatening?  
A: Usually manageable, but must be treated properly.

## Voice Short Response
Your heartbeat is irregular. Treatment helps control rhythm and reduce stroke risk.
