## Common Questions

Q: Why both surgeon and cardiologist involved?  
A: Complex blockage may need combined planning.

Q: How long hospital stay?  
A: Depends on treatment type and recovery.

Q: Is rehab necessary?  
A: It often improves recovery.

## Escalation Guidance
- Chest pain → Emergency
- Fever after surgery → Urgent review
- Breathlessness → Immediate help
