# Hospital Support and Care

## Cardiac Services
- Emergency chest pain care
- ECG and blood testing
- Observation unit / CCU
- Coronary angiography
- Cardiology consultation
- Follow-up clinic

## During Hospital Stay
- Continuous monitoring
- Repeat ECG if needed
- Blood pressure and oxygen checks
- Chest pain monitoring
- Medicines and nursing care

## Care Team
- Cardiologist
- Emergency doctor
- Nurses
- Technicians
- Pharmacist
- Dietitian

## Follow-Up Support
- Appointment scheduling
- Medicine guidance
- Report review
- Lifestyle counseling

## Common Questions

Q: How long may I stay admitted?  
A: Depends on symptoms, tests, and treatment response.

Q: When is follow-up?  
A: As mentioned in discharge summary.

Q: Where is billing?  
A: Reception / billing desk.

## Escalation Guidance
- Chest pain returns → Emergency immediately
- Breathlessness → Urgent review
- Medicine issue → Doctor / pharmacist

## Voice Short Response
Our team is monitoring your chest pain and helping prevent a heart attack.
