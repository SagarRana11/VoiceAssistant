# Hospital Support and Care

## Cardiac Services
- Emergency chest pain treatment
- Cardiac ICU / CCU
- Angiography
- Angioplasty / Stent
- Cardiology consultation
- Follow-up clinics

## During Hospital Stay
- Continuous monitoring
- ECG checks
- Blood pressure and oxygen checks
- Medicines and nursing care
- Doctor review

## Care Team
- Cardiologist
- Nurses
- Technicians
- Pharmacist
- Dietitian
- Physiotherapist

## Follow-Up Support
- Appointment scheduling
- Report review
- Medicine guidance
- Cardiac rehab referral

## Common Questions

Q: Where is billing?  
A: Please contact the billing desk / reception.

Q: Where is pharmacy?  
A: Ask front desk for pharmacy location.

Q: When is follow-up?  
A: As mentioned in discharge summary.

Q: Who to contact urgently?  
A: Emergency desk or nearest emergency room.

## Escalation Guidance
- Chest pain → Emergency immediately
- Bleeding → Hospital urgently
- Medicine issue → Doctor / pharmacist
- Appointment issue → Reception

Our cardiac team is available for treatment, recovery support, and follow-up care.
