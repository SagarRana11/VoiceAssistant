## Common Questions

Q: Why daily weight checks?  
A: To detect fluid buildup early.

Q: Why low salt diet?  
A: Helps reduce fluid retention.

Q: Can symptoms improve?  
A: Many patients improve with treatment.

## Escalation Guidance
- Severe breathlessness → Emergency
- Rapid swelling → Urgent review
- Fainting → Immediate help

## Voice Short Response
Our team helps control fluid buildup and support a weaker heart.
