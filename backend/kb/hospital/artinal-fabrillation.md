# Hospital Support and Care

## Cardiac Services
- ECG rhythm assessment
- Holter monitoring
- Cardioversion
- Ablation services
- Anticoagulation clinic
- Follow-up rhythm clinic

## During Hospital Stay
- Heart rhythm monitoring
- Pulse and BP checks
- Medicine adjustment
- Procedure preparation if needed

## Care Team
- Cardiologist
- Electrophysiology specialist
- Nurses
- Technicians
- Pharmacist

## Follow-Up Support
- ECG review
- Blood thinner guidance
- Rhythm symptom monitoring
- Procedure follow-up

## Common Questions

Q: Why repeated ECGs?  
A: To track rhythm changes.

Q: Why blood thinner clinic?  
A: To reduce stroke risk safely.

Q: Can AF return?  
A: Yes, follow-up is important.

## Escalation Guidance
- Stroke symptoms → Emergency
- Fainting → Immediate help
- Severe palpitations → Urgent review

## Voice Short Response
Our rhythm team helps control irregular heartbeat and reduce stroke risk.
