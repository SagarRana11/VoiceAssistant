# Hospital Support and Care

## Cardiac Services
- Cardiology consultation
- ECG and stress testing
- Coronary angiography
- Angioplasty / stent
- Preventive heart clinic
- Follow-up OPD

## During Hospital Stay
- Monitoring if admitted
- Test coordination
- Medicine management
- Doctor review

## Care Team
- Cardiologist
- Nurses
- Technicians
- Pharmacist
- Dietitian
- Rehabilitation staff

## Follow-Up Support
- Risk factor management
- BP / sugar monitoring
- Exercise counseling
- Repeat investigations

## Common Questions

Q: Do I need admission?  
A: Depends on symptoms and test results.

Q: Can I continue treatment as OPD?  
A: Many CAD patients can, depending on stability.

Q: Where is pharmacy?  
A: Ask front desk.

## Escalation Guidance
- New chest pain → Urgent review
- Reduced exercise tolerance → Doctor consult
- Medicine side effects → Contact team

## Voice Short Response
Our cardiac team helps manage blocked arteries and protect long-term heart health.
