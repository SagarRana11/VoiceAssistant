# Emergency Guidance – Coronary Artery Disease

## Seek Immediate Medical Help If

- New severe chest pain
- Chest pain during rest
- Pain not relieved by rest
- Breathlessness worsening suddenly
- Sweating with pain
- Fainting
- Sudden weakness

## What To Do Now

- Stop activity immediately
- Sit upright
- Use prescribed emergency medicine if advised
- Go to emergency room urgently

## Do Not Ignore
Stable CAD symptoms can become unstable suddenly.

## Voice Short Response
New chest pain or sudden breathlessness needs urgent medical review.
