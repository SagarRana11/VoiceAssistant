# Emergency Guidance – Triple Vessel Disease

## Seek Immediate Medical Help If

- Chest pain at rest
- Severe breathlessness
- Sweating with pain
- Fainting
- Sudden weakness
- Palpitations with collapse
- Fever after bypass surgery
- Wound redness with illness

## What To Do Now

- Stop activity
- Sit down
- Call emergency services
- Go to nearest emergency hospital

## Do Not Ignore

Multiple blocked arteries can lead to serious cardiac emergencies.

## Voice Short Response

Chest pain or severe breathlessness needs urgent emergency care now.
