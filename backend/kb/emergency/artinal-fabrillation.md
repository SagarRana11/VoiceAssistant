# Emergency Guidance – Atrial Fibrillation

## Seek Immediate Medical Help If

- Severe palpitations with dizziness
- Fainting
- Chest pain
- Severe breathlessness
- Stroke symptoms:
  - Face drooping
  - Arm weakness
  - Speech difficulty
- Heavy bleeding while on blood thinners

## What To Do Now

- Sit or lie safely
- Seek urgent medical care
- Call emergency services if stroke symptoms appear

## Do Not Ignore
AF can cause stroke or dangerous rapid heartbeat.

## Voice Short Response
Stroke symptoms, fainting, or severe palpitations need emergency help immediately.
