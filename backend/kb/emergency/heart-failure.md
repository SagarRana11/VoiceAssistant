# Emergency Guidance – Heart Failure

## Seek Immediate Medical Help If

- Severe breathlessness
- Unable to lie flat due to breathing difficulty
- Rapid swelling of legs or abdomen
- Sudden weight gain with symptoms
- Chest pain
- Fainting
- Blue lips or confusion

## What To Do Now

- Sit upright
- Use oxygen if prescribed
- Seek emergency hospital care
- Bring medicine list if possible

## Do Not Ignore
Heart failure can worsen quickly and may need urgent treatment.

## Voice Short Response
Severe breathing trouble or sudden swelling needs emergency care now.
