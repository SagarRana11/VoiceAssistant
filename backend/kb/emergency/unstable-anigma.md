# Emergency Guidance – Unstable Angina

## Seek Immediate Medical Help If

- Chest pain returns at rest
- Chest pain lasts more than 5 minutes
- Pain spreads to jaw, arm, or back
- Sweating with chest discomfort
- Severe breathlessness
- Fainting or near fainting
- Sudden weakness

## What To Do Now

- Stop activity and sit down
- Take prescribed chest pain medicine if advised
- Call emergency services / go to nearest hospital
- Do not drive yourself if severe symptoms

## Do Not Ignore
Unstable angina can quickly become a heart attack.

## Voice Short Response
Chest pain at rest can be dangerous. Please seek emergency care immediately.
