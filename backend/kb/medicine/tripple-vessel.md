# Triple Vessel Disease Medicines

## Why Medicines Matter
These medicines protect the heart before and after surgery or stent treatment.

## Antiplatelets

### Aspirin
Helps reduce clot risk.

### Clopidogrel / Ticagrelor
Used after stent or as advised.

## Statins

### Atorvastatin / Rosuvastatin
Strong cholesterol lowering is important.

## Beta Blockers
Reduce heart workload.

## ACE Inhibitors / ARB
Support heart function and blood pressure.

## Diabetes / BP Medicines
Often needed because many patients have multiple risk factors.

## Important Rules
- Take medicines daily
- Continue after surgery unless changed
- Do not stop blood thinners without advice

## Common Side Effects
- Bleeding
- Dizziness
- Muscle pain
- Low pulse

## Seek Help If
- Chest pain
- Heavy bleeding
- Severe weakness
- Breathlessness

## FAQ

Q: If surgery done, still need medicines?  
A: Yes, medicines remain important.

Q: Why strong statin needed?  
A: To reduce future artery blockage risk.

## Voice Short Response
These medicines protect your heart before and after major treatment.
