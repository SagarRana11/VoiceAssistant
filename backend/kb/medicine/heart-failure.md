# Heart Failure Medicines

## Why Medicines Matter
These medicines help the heart pump better, remove extra fluid, and reduce hospitalization risk.

## Diuretics

### Furosemide / Torsemide
Remove extra fluid and reduce swelling or breathlessness.

## ACE Inhibitors / ARB / ARNI
Help heart function and blood pressure.

## Beta Blockers
Reduce strain on the heart and improve long-term outcomes.

## Mineralocorticoid Medicines

### Spironolactone / Eplerenone
Used in selected patients to improve outcomes.

## SGLT2 Medicines
Helpful in many heart failure patients, even some without diabetes.

## Important Rules
- Take regularly
- Monitor weight daily
- Follow salt/fluid advice
- Do not stop suddenly

## Common Side Effects
- Dizziness
- Frequent urination
- High potassium
- Low blood pressure
- Kidney changes

## Seek Help If
- Rapid weight gain
- Severe breathlessness
- Fainting
- Very low urine output

## FAQ

Q: Why water tablet needed?  
A: It removes extra fluid.

Q: Can heart medicines improve function?  
A: Yes, many patients improve with treatment.

## Voice Short Response
These medicines help remove fluid and support a weaker heart.
