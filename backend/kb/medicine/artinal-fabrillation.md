# Atrial Fibrillation Medicines

## Why Medicines Matter
These medicines reduce stroke risk and control heart rate or rhythm.

## Blood Thinners

### Apixaban / Rivaroxaban / Dabigatran / Warfarin
Reduce clot formation and stroke risk.

## Rate Control Medicines

### Metoprolol / Bisoprolol
Slow fast heartbeat.

### Diltiazem / Verapamil
Used in selected patients.

## Rhythm Medicines

### Amiodarone / Flecainide / Others
Used to maintain normal rhythm in selected patients.

## Important Rules
- Never skip blood thinners
- Take rate medicines regularly
- Attend follow-up ECG tests

## Common Side Effects
- Bleeding
- Slow pulse
- Dizziness
- Fatigue

## Seek Help If
- Stroke symptoms
- Heavy bleeding
- Fainting
- Severe palpitations

## FAQ

Q: Why blood thinner needed?  
A: AF can form clots causing stroke.

Q: Can medicines cure AF?  
A: They help control rhythm and symptoms.

## Voice Short Response
These medicines reduce stroke risk and control irregular heartbeat.
