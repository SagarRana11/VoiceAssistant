# Unstable Angina Medicines

## Why Medicines Matter
These medicines reduce chest pain, prevent clot formation, and lower the chance of heart attack.

## Antiplatelets

### Aspirin
Helps prevent blood clots.

### Clopidogrel / Ticagrelor
Used with aspirin in many patients to reduce clot risk.

## Statins

### Atorvastatin / Rosuvastatin
Lower cholesterol and stabilize artery plaque.

## Beta Blockers

### Metoprolol / Bisoprolol
Reduce heart workload and control heart rate.

## ACE Inhibitors / ARB
Help control blood pressure and protect the heart.

## Nitrates

### Nitroglycerin
Used to relieve chest pain.

## Important Rules
- Take medicines regularly
- Do not stop without doctor advice
- Keep emergency chest pain medicine if prescribed

## Common Side Effects
- Bleeding
- Dizziness
- Low blood pressure
- Muscle pain
- Tiredness

## Seek Help If
- Chest pain continues
- Heavy bleeding
- Fainting
- Severe weakness

## FAQ

Q: Why many medicines?  
A: Each protects your heart in a different way.

Q: Can I stop when pain improves?  
A: No, continue unless advised.

## Voice Short Response
These medicines reduce clot risk, chest pain, and future heart problems.
