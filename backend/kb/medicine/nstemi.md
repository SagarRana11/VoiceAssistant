# STEMI Medicines Education

## Why Medicines Matter
These medicines help protect your heart, prevent new clots, reduce future heart attack risk, and improve recovery.

## Antiplatelets

### Aspirin
Prevents platelets from forming clots.

### Clopidogrel / Ticagrelor / Prasugrel
Helps keep the stent open and reduces repeat heart attack risk.

## Statins

### Atorvastatin / Rosuvastatin
Lower cholesterol and stabilize artery plaque.

## Beta Blockers

### Metoprolol / Bisoprolol
Reduce heart workload and control pulse.

## ACE Inhibitors / ARB

### Ramipril / Telmisartan etc.
Help control blood pressure and protect heart function.

## Nitrates
May be used for chest discomfort in selected patients.

## Important Rules
- Take medicines on time
- Do not stop medicines on your own
- Refill before stock ends
- Inform doctor before surgery or dental procedure

## Common Side Effects
- Bleeding / bruising
- Acidity
- Muscle pain
- Dizziness
- Slow pulse
- Dry cough (some medicines)

## Seek Medical Advice If
- Heavy bleeding
- Black stools
- Severe weakness
- Swelling
- Fainting
- Rash

## FAQ

Q: Why so many medicines?  
A: Each medicine protects your heart in a different way.

Q: Can I stop if I feel normal?  
A: No, continue unless doctor advises.

## Voice Short Response
These medicines protect your heart and help prevent another attack.
