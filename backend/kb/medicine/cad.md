# Coronary Artery Disease Medicines

## Why Medicines Matter
These medicines slow plaque buildup, improve blood flow, and reduce risk of heart attack.

## Antiplatelets

### Aspirin
Used in many CAD patients to reduce clot risk.

## Statins

### Atorvastatin / Rosuvastatin
Lower bad cholesterol and stabilize plaques.

## Beta Blockers
Reduce heart workload and chest pain.

## ACE Inhibitors / ARB
Help control blood pressure and protect heart/kidneys.

## Nitrates
Used when chest pain occurs.

## Other Medicines
- Calcium channel blockers in some patients
- Diabetes medicines if needed

## Important Rules
- Take medicines daily
- Control BP, sugar, cholesterol
- Do not self-stop treatment

## Common Side Effects
- Muscle pain
- Dizziness
- Low pulse
- Headache

## Seek Help If
- New chest pain
- Severe weakness
- Heavy bleeding
- Fainting

## FAQ

Q: Do medicines remove blockage?  
A: They help control disease and prevent worsening.

Q: Do all CAD patients need aspirin?  
A: Depends on doctor advice and bleeding risk.

## Voice Short Response
These medicines protect your arteries and lower heart attack risk.
