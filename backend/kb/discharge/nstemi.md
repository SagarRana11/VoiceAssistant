# STEMI Discharge Education

## What Happened
You had a heart attack caused by sudden blockage of a heart artery.

## What Was Done
Treatment such as angioplasty and stent was performed to restore blood flow.

## Your Recovery Goal
Protect the heart, prevent another attack, regain strength safely.

## Medicines
- Take all medicines exactly as prescribed
- Do not stop aspirin or blood thinners without doctor advice
- Keep medicine timings regular

## Activity Guidance
- Start light walking if advised
- Increase activity gradually
- Avoid heavy lifting initially
- Take rest when tired

## Diet Guidance
- Low salt diet
- Low oil and fried food
- Eat fruits and vegetables
- Reduce sugar if diabetic
- Avoid smoking
- Limit alcohol if advised

## Wound Care
Keep wrist/groin site clean and dry. Report swelling or bleeding.

## Follow-Up Plan
- Visit doctor in 3 to 7 days or as advised
- Bring reports and medicine list
- Repeat tests if recommended

## Cardiac Rehabilitation
Structured exercise and recovery program may be advised.

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent help if:
- Chest pain returns
- Severe breathlessness
- Fainting
- Fast or irregular heartbeat
- Bleeding
- Sudden weakness

## Emotional Recovery
Some anxiety after heart attack is common. Discuss concerns with doctor or family.

## Teach Back Questions
- Which medicines must not be stopped?
- When should you seek emergency help?
- When is your follow-up visit?

## FAQ

Q: When can I return to work?  
A: Depends on recovery and job type. Ask your doctor.

Q: Can I travel?  
A: Usually later, after doctor approval.

## Voice Short Response
Take medicines regularly, walk lightly, eat healthy, and seek help if chest pain returns.
