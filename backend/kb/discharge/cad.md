# Coronary Artery Disease Discharge Education

## What Happened
Your heart arteries are narrowed, reducing blood flow to the heart.

## What Was Done
You were treated with medicines, testing, and in some cases angiography or stent treatment.

## Recovery Goal
Reduce symptoms, prevent heart attack, and slow disease progression.

## Medicines
- Take medicines daily
- Control blood pressure, sugar, cholesterol
- Carry angina medicines if prescribed

## Activity Guidance
- Walk regularly
- Avoid sudden overexertion
- Exercise as advised

## Diet Guidance
- Low cholesterol diet
- Low salt
- Weight control
- Stop smoking

## Follow-Up Plan
- Cardiology review as advised
- Repeat tests if needed
- Risk factor monitoring

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent help if:
- New chest pain
- Pain on mild activity
- Breathlessness worsening
- Fainting

- When should chest pain be checked urgently?

## FAQ

Q: Can blockage worsen silently?  
A: Yes, follow-up is important.

Q: Is exercise safe?  
A: Usually yes, if guided.

## Voice Short Response
Take medicines, stay active safely, and protect your arteries daily.
