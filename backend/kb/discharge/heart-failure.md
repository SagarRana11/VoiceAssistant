# Heart Failure Discharge Education

## What Happened
Your heart is weaker and not pumping blood efficiently.

## What Was Done
You were treated with medicines to remove fluid and support heart function.

## Recovery Goal
Improve breathing, reduce swelling, and prevent re-hospitalization.

## Medicines
- Take medicines exactly as prescribed
- Do not skip water tablets
- Monitor side effects

## Activity Guidance
- Gentle walking
- Rest when tired
- Gradually increase movement

## Diet Guidance
- Low salt diet
- Fluid restriction if advised
- Weight monitoring daily

## Follow-Up Plan
- Early review in 3 to 7 days or as advised
- Heart failure clinic if available

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent help if:
- Severe breathlessness
- Rapid weight gain
- Leg swelling worsening
- Fainting
- Chest pain

## Teach Back Questions
- Why weigh yourself daily?
- What signs mean fluid buildup?

## FAQ

Q: Why sudden weight gain matters?  
A: It may mean fluid retention.

Q: Can symptoms improve?  
A: Yes, many patients improve with treatment.

## Voice Short Response
Take medicines, watch weight, reduce salt, and seek help if breathing worsens.
