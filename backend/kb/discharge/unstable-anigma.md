# Unstable Angina Discharge Education

## What Happened
You had warning chest pain caused by reduced blood flow to the heart.

## What Was Done
You were treated with monitoring, medicines, and tests to reduce the risk of heart attack.

## Recovery Goal
Prevent heart attack, control symptoms, and improve long-term heart health.

## Medicines
- Take all medicines regularly
- Keep chest pain medicine available if prescribed
- Do not stop medicines without doctor advice

## Activity Guidance
- Light walking as advised
- Avoid heavy exertion initially
- Increase activity gradually

## Diet Guidance
- Low salt diet
- Low oil / fried foods
- More fruits and vegetables
- Stop smoking

## Follow-Up Plan
- Visit doctor in 3 to 7 days or as advised
- Review reports and medicines

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent help if:
- Chest pain returns
- Pain at rest
- Breathlessness
- Sweating with chest discomfort
- Fainting

## Teach Back Questions
- What will you do if chest pain returns?
- Which medicines are important daily?

## FAQ

Q: Can unstable angina become heart attack?  
A: Yes, urgent follow-up is important.

Q: Can I walk?  
A: Yes, gradually as advised.

## Voice Short Response
Take medicines, avoid strain, and seek help if chest pain returns.
