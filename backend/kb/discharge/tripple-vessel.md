# Triple Vessel Disease Discharge Education

## What Happened
Three major heart arteries have significant blockages.

## What Was Done
You were treated with medicines, stents, or bypass surgery depending on your condition.

## Recovery Goal
Restore strength, protect heart function, and prevent future cardiac events.

## Medicines
- Take all medicines daily
- Continue blood thinners if prescribed
- Strong cholesterol treatment is important

## Activity Guidance
- Walking rehabilitation
- Avoid strain initially
- Follow surgery or stent recovery advice

## Diet Guidance
- Low salt
- Low fat
- Diabetes control if needed
- Stop smoking

## Follow-Up Plan
- Cardiology review
- Surgical follow-up if CABG done
- Risk factor monitoring

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent help if:
- Chest pain
- Breathlessness
- Fever after surgery
- Wound redness
- Fainting

## Teach Back Questions
- Why are medicines still needed after treatment?
- When should you seek urgent help?

## FAQ

Q: If surgery done, am I cured?  
A: Treatment helps greatly, but lifelong care remains important.

Q: Can I return to normal life?  
A: Many patients do with rehabilitation.

## Voice Short Response
Take medicines, recover gradually, and protect your heart long term.
