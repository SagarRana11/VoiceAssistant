# Atrial Fibrillation Discharge Education

## What Happened
Your heart rhythm became irregular.

## What Was Done
You were treated with medicines or procedures to control rhythm or heart rate.

## Recovery Goal
Maintain safe rhythm control and reduce stroke risk.

## Medicines
- Never skip blood thinners
- Take pulse/rhythm medicines regularly
- Attend follow-up tests

## Activity Guidance
- Light activity usually allowed
- Avoid overexertion initially
- Resume gradually

## Diet Guidance
- Limit alcohol
- Maintain healthy weight
- Reduce stimulants if advised

## Follow-Up Plan
- ECG follow-up
- Cardiology review
- Blood tests if needed

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent help if:
- Stroke symptoms
- Severe palpitations
- Fainting
- Breathlessness
- Heavy bleeding

## Teach Back Questions
- Why are blood thinners important?
- What are stroke warning signs?

## FAQ

Q: Can rhythm become irregular again?  
A: Yes, recurrence can happen.

Q: Should I stop blood thinners if rhythm feels normal?  
A: No, only doctor should decide.

## Voice Short Response
Take blood thinners regularly and seek help for stroke symptoms or severe palpitations.
