# Atrial Fibrillation Consent Education

## Purpose of Consent
Consent means understanding the planned rhythm treatment and agreeing after explanation.

## Why Procedure Is Needed
Your heart rhythm is irregular. Doctors may recommend cardioversion or ablation to restore rhythm or improve symptoms.

## What Is Cardioversion
A controlled electric shock under sedation to reset heart rhythm.

## What Is Ablation
Thin catheters are guided into the heart to treat areas causing abnormal rhythm.

## Benefits
- Improves rhythm
- Reduces symptoms
- Improves quality of life
- May reduce hospital visits

## Common Risks
- Bleeding
- Sedation reaction
- Arrhythmia recurrence
- Rare stroke
- Rare heart injury

## Alternatives
- Medicines only
- Rate control strategy
- Observation depending on symptoms

## Patient Rights
- Ask questions
- Accept or decline treatment
- Seek second opinion

## Teach Back Questions
- Do you understand why rhythm treatment is advised?
- Would you like the steps repeated?

## Sample Consent Statement
I understand the proposed rhythm treatment and its risks.

## Voice Short Response
This treatment aims to improve your heart rhythm and symptoms.
