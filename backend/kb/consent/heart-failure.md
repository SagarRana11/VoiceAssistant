# Heart Failure Consent Education

## Purpose of Consent
Consent means understanding the planned procedure or treatment for heart failure.

## Why Procedure Is Needed
Doctors may recommend tests or procedures to identify the cause of heart weakness or improve heart function.

## Possible Procedures
- Coronary angiography
- Device implantation (ICD / CRT)
- Valve procedure
- Advanced monitoring

## What Is Angiography
Checks if blocked arteries caused heart weakness.

## What Is ICD / CRT
A device placed under the skin may prevent dangerous rhythms or improve pumping coordination.

## Benefits
- Finds cause of heart failure
- Improves symptoms
- Prevents complications
- May improve survival

## Common Risks
- Bleeding
- Infection
- Device complications
- Irregular heartbeat
- Rare serious complications

## Alternatives
- Medicines only
- Observation
- Delayed intervention depending on condition

## Patient Rights
- Ask questions
- Understand options
- Accept or refuse treatment

## Teach Back Questions
- Do you understand why this treatment is advised?
- Would you like device risks explained again?

## Sample Consent Statement
I understand the proposed treatment and agree after explanation.

## Voice Short Response
This treatment may help your heart pump better and reduce complications.
