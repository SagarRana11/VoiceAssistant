# Unstable Angina Consent Education

## Purpose of Consent
Consent means understanding the recommended treatment, benefits, risks, and alternatives before agreeing.

## Why Procedure Is Needed
You have warning chest pain due to reduced blood flow to the heart. Doctors recommend angiography to identify blockage and prevent heart attack.

## What Is Angiography
A catheter is inserted through wrist or groin and guided to the heart arteries. Dye is used to see blockages.

## What Is Angioplasty / PCI
If blockage is found, balloon treatment and stent placement may be done.

## Benefits
- Finds blockage quickly
- Improves blood flow
- Prevents heart attack
- Relieves chest pain
- Guides future treatment

## Common Risks
- Bleeding
- Bruising
- Contrast allergy
- Kidney stress
- Irregular heartbeat
- Rare stroke
- Rare emergency surgery

## Alternatives
- Medicine-only treatment in selected patients
- Delayed procedure depending on risk
- Observation with tests

## Patient Rights
- Ask questions anytime
- Accept or refuse treatment
- Request second opinion if feasible

## Teach Back Questions
- Do you understand why doctors recommend angiography?
- Would you like risks explained again?

## Sample Consent Statement
I understand the proposed procedure, risks, benefits, and alternatives.

## Voice Short Response
This test checks heart arteries and helps prevent a heart attack.
