# Triple Vessel Disease Consent Education

## Purpose of Consent
Consent means understanding the treatment plan for severe multi-vessel heart blockage.

## Why Procedure Is Needed
Three major heart arteries have significant blockage. Doctors recommend urgent or planned revascularization.

## Possible Procedures
- Coronary angiography
- Angioplasty in selected cases
- Bypass surgery (CABG)

## What Is CABG
Surgeons create new pathways for blood flow using graft vessels.

## What Is PCI
Blocked arteries may be opened using balloon and stents.

## Benefits
- Improves blood flow
- Relieves angina
- Protects heart function
- Reduces future risk

## Common Risks
- Bleeding
- Infection
- Stroke
- Irregular heartbeat
- Kidney stress
- Rare serious complications

## Alternatives
- Intensive medicines only in selected patients
- Delayed surgery depending on risk

## Patient Rights
- Ask questions
- Discuss with family
- Accept or refuse treatment

## Teach Back Questions
- Do you understand why major treatment is advised?
- Would you like CABG and PCI differences explained?

## Sample Consent Statement
I understand the proposed treatment plan and risks.

## Voice Short Response
This treatment improves blood flow in multiple blocked heart arteries.
