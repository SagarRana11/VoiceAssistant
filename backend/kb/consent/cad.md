# Coronary Artery Disease Consent Education

## Purpose of Consent
Consent ensures you understand the planned test or treatment before proceeding.

## Why Procedure Is Needed
Doctors suspect narrowed heart arteries and recommend angiography to assess blockages. Treatment may include angioplasty or surgery planning.

## What Is Angiography
A catheter reaches the heart arteries through wrist or groin. Dye shows narrowing on imaging.

## What Is Angioplasty / PCI
A balloon may open narrowed areas and a stent may be inserted.

## Benefits
- Identifies blockage severity
- Improves blood flow
- Reduces angina symptoms
- Helps treatment planning

## Common Risks
- Bleeding
- Bruising
- Dye reaction
- Kidney stress
- Irregular heartbeat
- Rare stroke

## Alternatives
- Continue medicines only
- Stress test / CT scan in selected cases
- Delayed procedure

## Patient Rights
- Ask questions
- Discuss with family
- Accept or decline treatment

## Teach Back Questions
- Do you understand why blockage testing is advised?
- Do you have concerns about the procedure?

## Sample Consent Statement
I understand the proposed heart artery evaluation and possible treatment.

## Voice Short Response
This procedure checks narrowed arteries and may improve blood flow.
