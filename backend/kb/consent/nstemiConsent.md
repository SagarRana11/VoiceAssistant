# NSTEMI Consent Education

## Why Treatment Is Needed

You have signs of heart muscle damage due to reduced blood flow. Treatment helps prevent a bigger heart attack.

## Recommended Procedure

Coronary Angiography with possible Angioplasty/Stent.

## What Is Angiography?

A thin tube is inserted through wrist or groin to check heart arteries using dye and X-ray imaging.

## Possible Benefits

- Finds blockage quickly
- Restores blood flow
- Reduces chest pain
- Prevents complications

## Risks Explained

- Bleeding
- Bruising
- Allergy to dye
- Kidney stress
- Irregular heartbeat
- Rare emergency surgery
- Rare stroke / death

## Alternatives

- Medicine-only treatment in selected cases
- Further tests depending on doctor advice

## Understanding Confirmation

Please ask any doubts before consent.

## Consent Note

Final consent must be taken by treating doctor/hospital policy.
