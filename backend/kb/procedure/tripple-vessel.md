# Triple Vessel Disease Procedure Guidance

## Goal
Improve blood flow to multiple blocked heart arteries and protect heart function.

## Before Procedure
- Coronary angiography
- ECG
- Blood tests
- Surgical evaluation if needed
- Consent completed

## Possible Procedures
- CABG surgery
- PCI in selected cases
- Intensive medical therapy

## During CABG
1. General anesthesia given
2. Chest opened surgically
3. Graft vessels used to bypass blockages
4. Blood flow restored
5. ICU transfer after surgery

## During PCI (Selected Cases)
1. Catheter inserted
2. Multiple arteries treated with stents if suitable

## Equipment Used
- Surgical tools
- Heart-lung support if required
- Catheters
- Stents
- ICU monitors

## Typical Duration
CABG: 3 to 6 hours  
PCI: 1 to 3 hours

## Will It Hurt
You are asleep during surgery. Pain control is provided after.

## After Procedure
- ICU / ward monitoring
- Breathing exercises
- Walking rehabilitation
- Continue medicines

## Possible Complications
- Bleeding
- Infection
- Stroke
- Arrhythmia
- Kidney stress

## Red Flags After Procedure
- Chest pain
- Fever
- Wound redness
- Breathlessness
- Fainting

## FAQ

Q: Why surgery instead of stent?  
A: Surgery may give better results in complex multi-vessel disease.

Q: How long recovery takes?  
A: Recovery may take weeks.

## Voice Short Response
Treatment restores blood flow in several blocked heart arteries.
