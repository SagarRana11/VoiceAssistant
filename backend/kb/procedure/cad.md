# Coronary Artery Disease Procedure Guidance

## Goal
Assess narrowed heart arteries and improve blood flow when needed.

## Before Procedure
- Clinical review
- ECG
- Blood tests
- Consent completed
- Kidney function checked
- IV line started

## During Procedure
1. Local anesthesia given
2. Catheter inserted through wrist or groin
3. Guided to heart arteries
4. Dye injected to view narrowing
5. Balloon and stent may be used
6. Final blood flow confirmed

## Equipment Used
- Catheter
- Guidewire
- Balloon
- Stent
- Imaging system

## Typical Duration
30 minutes to 2 hours.

## Will It Hurt
Mostly pressure sensation or mild discomfort.

## After Procedure
- Recovery monitoring
- Site inspection
- Gradual mobilization
- Continue medicines

## Possible Complications
- Bleeding
- Bruising
- Kidney stress
- Arrhythmia
- Rare emergency surgery

## Red Flags After Procedure
- Chest pain
- Bleeding
- Limb numbness
- Breathlessness

## FAQ

Q: Will every blockage be stented?  
A: Not always. It depends on severity and location.

Q: Can surgery be advised instead?  
A: Yes, in complex disease.

## Voice Short Response
Doctors examine narrowed arteries and may open them with a stent.
