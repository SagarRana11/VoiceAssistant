# Heart Failure Procedure Guidance

## Goal
Find the cause of heart weakness and improve heart function.

## Before Procedure
- Symptom review
- ECG
- Echocardiography
- Blood tests
- Consent completed
- Monitoring started

## Possible Procedures
- Coronary angiography
- ICD implantation
- CRT device placement
- Valve intervention
- Advanced imaging

## During Procedure (Example Device Placement)
1. Local anesthesia given
2. Leads inserted through vein
3. Device connected under skin
4. Rhythm tested
5. Wound closed

## Equipment Used
- ECG monitor
- Imaging system
- ICD / CRT device
- Leads
- Catheter tools

## Typical Duration
1 to 3 hours depending on procedure.

## Will It Hurt
Local anesthesia reduces pain. Mild soreness later is common.

## After Procedure
- Rhythm monitoring
- Wound checks
- Continue medicines
- Device review if implanted

## Possible Complications
- Bleeding
- Infection
- Lead movement
- Arrhythmia
- Rare lung injury

## Red Flags After Procedure
- Fever
- Swelling at wound
- Breathlessness
- Fainting

## FAQ

Q: Why device needed?  
A: It may improve pumping or prevent dangerous rhythms.

Q: Is recovery long?  
A: Many patients recover in a few days.

## Voice Short Response
This procedure helps identify causes or support a weak heart.
